# Productization research notes

## Arabic resource

The University of Illinois Department of Linguistics page presents a curated collection of Arabic learning resources, including general resources, dictionaries, vocabulary, and grammar links. It is suitable as a **curated external directory**, not as a curriculum authority or an automatically verified source for generated answers: https://linguistics.illinois.edu/languages/arabic/student-resources/arabic-online-resources

## Mathematics resource

The U.S. National Science Foundation page presents classroom-oriented mathematics lessons and web resources, including activities and platforms for grades K–12 or specified grade bands. It is suitable as a **public reference directory**; the product must still show the specific link and require teacher review rather than claim that a generated answer is curriculum-verified: https://www.nsf.gov/focus-areas/mathematics/educational-resources

## Product decision

The assistant will distinguish teacher-provided context from external reference links. No source is treated as curriculum-grounded merely because it appears in a curated directory. Any future retrieval must preserve the exact URL, retrieval time, and review status; the current bounded implementation does not yet perform automatic retrieval.
